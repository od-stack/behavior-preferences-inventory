Rate each situation by thinking about how a person approaches their working life. Each of the 12 groups has four statements to score.

How to score each group
  • Give 0 to the statement that fits least and 6 to the one that fits most.
  • Give the other two statements a score from 1 to 5. The two scores must be different.
  • Every score can be used only once within a group.
